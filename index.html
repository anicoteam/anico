Anime app FAQ

What is ANICO?

ANICO is an Android anime streaming app focused on discovery, playback, and schedule, with a dark cinematic interface.

Is ANICO free?

The app is free to install. Optional account sign-in is available. Browsing and watching do not require social login.

Which devices are supported?

Android only. There is no iOS build at this time.

Does ANICO support subtitles?

Yes. Episodes support SUB playback. DUB is shown when it is available for that episode.

Can I create a watchlist?

You can save continue-watching progress on your device. Optional sign-in helps sync your profile across sessions. My List and the smart watchlist cover favorites and a queue.
